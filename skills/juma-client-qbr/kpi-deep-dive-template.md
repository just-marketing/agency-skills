# KPI Deep Dive Template

Use this structure for each KPI that needs detailed explanation in the QBR. Every KPI categorized as "Watch" or "Off Track" requires a deep dive. "On Track" KPIs should also get a deep dive if there is an important story behind the success.

---

## [KPI Name]: [On Track / Watch / Off Track]

**What happened:** [Factual performance summary with monthly breakdown showing the quarterly arc. Do not hide behind quarterly averages -- if Month 1 was terrible and Month 3 was great, show the real trajectory.]

**Why:** [Root cause analysis with data support. Correlate with known activities: campaigns launched, budget changes, algorithm updates, seasonal patterns, competitor moves, or client-side changes.]

**What we are doing:** [Specific actions, timeline, expected impact. For Off Track KPIs, include a recovery plan. For On Track KPIs, explain how to sustain or accelerate.]

---

## Usage Notes

- Show the monthly trend within the quarter so the client sees the real trajectory
- For every KPI, calculate: quarterly target, quarterly actual, variance, and trend direction
- Categorize each KPI as On Track, Watch, or Off Track
- For Off Track KPIs, prepare a root cause analysis and recovery plan
- For On Track KPIs, identify what is driving success and whether it is sustainable
- Connect performance to business impact, not just channel metrics
