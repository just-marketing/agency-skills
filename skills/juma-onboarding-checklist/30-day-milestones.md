## Audit Scheduling Guide

Schedule audits in the appropriate order based on contracted services:

| Timeframe | Audit Type | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Week 1-2 (Foundation)** | Analytics audit, brand/messaging review | Verify tracking accuracy, understand brand |
| **Week 2-3 (Channel-specific)** | Channel audit, SEO audit, paid media audit, social audit | Assess current performance per channel |
| **Week 3-4 (Strategic)** | Competitive analysis, content audit, conversion rate review | Inform strategy development |

---

## Internal Weekly Sync

Hold a brief internal team sync at the end of each week to review:
- What was completed this week?
- What is blocked and needs escalation?
- What is the priority for next week?
- Are we on track for the 30-day milestones?
