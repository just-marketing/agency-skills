## Scoring Guide

### Profitability (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Effective hourly rate and margin significantly above agency benchmarks |
| 4 | Above benchmark -- healthy and sustainable |
| 3 | At benchmark -- acceptable but no cushion |
| 2 | Below benchmark -- margin pressure, needs attention |
| 1 | Significantly below benchmark -- losing money on this account |

### Deliverable Completion (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | 95%+ on-time completion, client consistently impressed with quality |
| 4 | 85-95% on-time, minor delays resolved quickly |
| 3 | 75-85% on-time, occasional missed deadlines |
| 2 | 60-75% on-time, recurring delivery issues |
| 1 | Below 60% on-time, systemic delivery problems |

### Client Satisfaction (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Enthusiastic client, gives referrals, expands scope proactively |
| 4 | Satisfied, responsive, positive feedback, attends all meetings |
| 3 | Neutral -- no complaints but no enthusiasm either |
| 2 | Showing warning signs: slower responses, missed meetings, occasional criticism |
| 1 | Actively dissatisfied, escalations, churn risk is high |

### Scope Discipline (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | All work is within SOW, no scope creep, clean boundaries |
| 4 | Minor out-of-scope requests handled appropriately |
| 3 | Some recurring scope creep but manageable |
| 2 | Significant scope creep impacting profitability |
| 1 | Extensive uncompensated out-of-scope work, SOW is meaningless |

### Team Health (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Team is engaged, appropriately allocated, right seniority mix |
| 4 | Minor allocation issues but team is stable and effective |
| 3 | Some overallocation or seniority mismatch, manageable |
| 2 | Team members overworked on this account, retention risk |
| 1 | Burnout, single points of failure, team actively wants off the account |

---

## Status Thresholds

| Overall Average | Status | Action |
|----------------|--------|--------|
| 4.0 - 5.0 | **Healthy** | Maintain current approach, look for expansion opportunities |
| 3.0 - 3.9 | **Needs Attention** | Address specific low-scoring dimensions, monitor monthly |
| 2.0 - 2.9 | **At Risk** | Immediate intervention needed, escalate to leadership |
| 1.0 - 1.9 | **Critical** | Emergency review, consider renegotiation or managed exit |
