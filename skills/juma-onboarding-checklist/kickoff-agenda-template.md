## Agenda

### 1. Introductions (10 min)
- Agency team members and their roles
- Client team members and their roles
- Who is the day-to-day contact on each side
- Who has decision-making and approval authority

### 2. Goals Alignment and Success Metrics (15 min)
- Review the SOW objectives and confirm priority order
- Discuss how success will be measured (specific KPIs)
- Confirm baseline metrics and current performance
- Align on realistic timelines for seeing results

### 3. Communication Preferences and Cadence (10 min)
- Preferred communication channels (email, Slack, Teams)
- Response time expectations (both directions)
- Escalation path for urgent issues
- Standing meeting schedule (weekly/biweekly status, monthly/quarterly reviews)

### 4. Quick Wins Discussion (10 min)
- Discuss 2-3 things the agency can deliver or improve in the first two weeks
- Build momentum and confidence early
- Identify any low-hanging fruit visible from initial review

### 5. First-30-Days Timeline Review (10 min)
- Walk through the week-by-week plan
- Confirm key milestone dates
- Identify any known scheduling constraints or blackout dates
- Review the access provisioning checklist and confirm the client's process for granting access

### 6. Open Questions and Next Steps (5 min)
- Address any client concerns
- Clarify ambiguities in the SOW
- Confirm immediate action items and owners
- Set the date for the first status meeting

---

## Post-Kickoff Actions

- [ ] Document all decisions, action items, and open questions from the kickoff
- [ ] Distribute the kickoff summary to all attendees within 24 hours
- [ ] Create action items in the project management platform with owners and deadlines
- [ ] Send access provisioning requests if not already sent
- [ ] Schedule the first status meeting if not already on the calendar
