# Deliverable Specification Template

## Writing Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria must be objective and testable, not subjective. They answer: "How will both parties know this deliverable is complete and satisfactory?"

### Good Acceptance Criteria Examples
- "Design delivered in Figma, responsive across 3 breakpoints, incorporating brand guidelines from client context"
- "Blog post of 1,500-2,000 words, targeting [keyword], including 3 internal links and 2 CTAs"
- "Campaign launched on Google Ads and Meta with minimum 3 ad variations per audience segment"

### Bad Acceptance Criteria Examples
- "Client is happy with the design" (subjective)
- "High-quality content" (unmeasurable)
- "Campaign performs well" (no objective standard)

## Deliverable Decomposition Checklist

When breaking proposal deliverables into SOW work items:
- [ ] Each deliverable is a single, trackable work item
- [ ] Format and specifications are explicit
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are objective and testable by a third party
- [ ] Hours are estimated (not open-ended)
- [ ] Delivery date is set
- [ ] Dependencies on other deliverables or client inputs are documented
