# Positioning Map Template

Use this template to visually plot the client and competitors on a two-axis positioning map. The map reveals whitespace opportunities and helps clients understand their relative market position at a glance.

---

## Positioning Map

```
[Axis Label: e.g., Technical -------> Accessible]

                HIGH [Axis 2, e.g., Premium]
                    |
    [Comp A]        |          [Comp B]
                    |
                    |     [Client]
                    |
LOW [Axis 1] ------+------ HIGH [Axis 1]
                    |
         [Comp C]   |
                    |    [Comp D]
                    |
                LOW [Axis 2, e.g., Value]
```

**Positioning Interpretation:** [2-3 sentences explaining where the client sits relative to competitors and what whitespace exists on the map.]

---

## Choosing the Right Axes

Select two axes that are most relevant to the client's industry and strategic decisions. Common axis pairs include:

| Axis Pair | Best For |
|-----------|----------|
| **Price/Value vs. Premium** | Product companies, SaaS, e-commerce |
| **Technical vs. Accessible** | B2B software, professional services |
| **Innovative vs. Traditional** | Disrupting industries, startups vs. incumbents |
| **Niche/Specialized vs. Broad/Generalist** | Professional services, agencies |
| **Self-Serve vs. High-Touch** | SaaS, service businesses |
| **Enterprise vs. SMB** | B2B companies, vertical SaaS |
| **Local/Regional vs. National/Global** | Retail, services, franchises |

## Guidelines

1. **Choose axes that create meaningful separation** -- if all competitors cluster in one quadrant, try different axes
2. **Use axes that reflect actual buyer decision criteria** -- the axes should represent how customers evaluate options
3. **Plot based on market perception, not self-description** -- use observed messaging, pricing, and audience signals
4. **Identify whitespace** -- empty quadrants or gaps between clusters represent positioning opportunities for the client
5. **Include the client on the map** -- show their current position and, optionally, their aspirational position with an arrow
