### Content Types That Get Cited

Prioritize creating content in formats that LLMs frequently cite:

| Content Type | Citation Frequency | Why It Works |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Definitive guides** | High | Comprehensive coverage makes them authoritative sources |
| **Original research/data** | High | Unique data cannot be found elsewhere |
| **Comparison pages** | High | Directly answers comparison queries |
| **FAQ hubs** | High | Structured Q&A format is easy for LLMs to extract |
| **How-to content** | Medium-High | Step-by-step format matches problem/solution queries |
| **Glossary/terminology pages** | Medium | Factual definitions are frequently cited |
