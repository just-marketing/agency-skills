## Prioritization Matrix

```
                    HIGH IMPACT
                        |
    Plan & Pitch        |       Pitch Now
    [opportunities]     |       [opportunities]
                        |
  LOW LIKELIHOOD -------+------- HIGH LIKELIHOOD
                        |
    Backlog             |       Easy Add-Ons
    [opportunities]     |       [opportunities]
                        |
                    LOW IMPACT
```

- **Pitch Now** (High Likelihood + High Impact): Lead with these in the next client conversation
- **Easy Add-Ons** (High Likelihood + Low Impact): Bundle with other recommendations or propose during routine check-ins
- **Plan & Pitch** (Low Likelihood + High Impact): Build the case over time, wait for the right trigger
- **Backlog** (Low Likelihood + Low Impact): Document but do not actively pursue
