# Channel Performance Template

Use this template for each active channel section in a client performance report. Every channel section follows the same structure: status indicator, metrics table, analysis, and recommendation.

---

**Recommendation:** [Specific next step with expected impact]

---

## Channel-Specific Metric Sets

### Organic Search
- Sessions, Conversions, Revenue/Leads, Avg. Position (target keywords)

### Paid Search
- Spend, Impressions, Clicks / CTR, Conversions / Conv. Rate, CPA, ROAS

### Paid Social
- Spend, Reach / Impressions, Clicks / CTR, Conversions / Conv. Rate, CPA, ROAS

### Organic Social
- Followers (net new), Engagement Rate, Reach, Link Clicks to Site
- Include **Top Performing Content** (top 3 posts with key metrics)

### Email Marketing
- Emails Sent, Open Rate, Click Rate, Unsubscribe Rate, Revenue from Email

---

## Analysis Guidelines

- Behind every significant metric movement is a story. Correlate performance changes with known activities: campaigns launched, budget changes, algorithm updates, seasonal patterns, competitor moves, or client-side changes.
- Translate data into business impact: "Organic traffic increased 23% driven by the pillar content strategy launched in Month 2, contributing an estimated $[X] in pipeline value."
- For every underperforming area, include a specific remediation plan with timeline.
- For every outperforming area, include a plan to double down or replicate the success.
