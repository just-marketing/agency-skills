# Situation Analysis Framework

Use this framework to synthesize client research into a compelling narrative that moves from current state to urgency to act.

## Current State

[Describe where the client is today based on research. Be specific - reference their actual channels, campaigns, market position. Show you understand their business.]

Key questions to answer:
- What channels are they currently active on?
- What is their current market position?
- What does their marketing presence look like objectively?
- Where are the observable gaps between their goals and current reality?

## Challenges

[Identify 3-5 specific challenges with evidence. Frame each as a solvable problem, not a criticism.]

For each challenge:
- State the challenge specifically (with data where possible)
- Provide evidence or observable proof
- Frame it as an opportunity to improve, not a failure

## Opportunities

[Map each challenge to a quantified opportunity. Use industry benchmarks, competitor data, or market trends to size the opportunity.]

For each opportunity:
- Connect it directly to one or more challenges
- Quantify the potential impact (revenue, leads, market share, efficiency)
- Reference industry benchmarks or competitor performance as proof points

## Cost of Inaction

[What happens if they do nothing? Quantify lost revenue, market share erosion, or competitive disadvantage over 6-12 months.]

Key dimensions to quantify:
- Revenue at risk from current trajectory
- Market share being captured by competitors
- Compounding effect of delayed action (e.g., SEO gap widening over time)
- Competitive disadvantage acceleration
- Internal team burnout or inefficiency costs
