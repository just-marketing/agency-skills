# Platform Selection Guide

Use this matrix to evaluate and select paid media platforms based on client objectives, audience, and budget. For each selected platform, document why it was chosen and its role in the media mix.

## Platform Comparison Matrix

| Platform | Best For | Consider When |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Google Search** | High-intent capture | Client's category has search volume |
| **Google Display/YouTube** | Awareness, retargeting | Need broad reach or video storytelling |
| **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** | B2C, ecommerce, lead gen | Visual products, broad targeting |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, professional services | Targeting by job title, company, industry |
| **TikTok** | Gen Z/Millennial, awareness | Brand has or can create short-form video |
| **Programmatic** | Scale, retargeting | Need reach beyond walled gardens |
| **Microsoft Ads** | B2B, desktop-heavy audiences | Lower CPCs, older demographic |

## Selection Criteria

When choosing platforms, evaluate each against these factors:

1. **Audience presence**: Is the target audience active on this platform? What is the estimated audience size?
2. **Objective alignment**: Does the platform's ad product support the campaign objective (awareness, consideration, conversion)?
3. **Budget viability**: Is there enough budget to generate statistically significant data on this platform? (Do not spread $5K across 5 platforms.)
4. **Creative capability**: Can the client produce the creative formats this platform requires?
5. **Competitive landscape**: Are competitors active here? Is there an opportunity or is the space saturated?
6. **Attribution clarity**: Can conversions from this platform be tracked and attributed reliably?

## Platform Role Mapping

For each selected platform, document:

- **Platform name**
- **Role in the media mix** (e.g., demand capture, awareness, retargeting, nurture)
- **Monthly budget allocation** and percentage of total spend
- **Primary KPI** for this platform
- **Why selected**: 1-2 sentences connecting the platform to the client's goals and audience
