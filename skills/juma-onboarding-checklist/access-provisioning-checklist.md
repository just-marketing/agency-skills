## Access Provisioning Best Practices

1. **Request all access in the welcome email**: Do not trickle requests over days and weeks
2. **Prefer agency manager accounts**: Use Business Manager (Meta), MCC (Google), etc. over direct credentials
3. **Verify permissions, not just access**: Confirm the agency has the right permission level (admin, editor, viewer)
4. **Store credentials securely**: Use the agency's credential manager -- never email, chat, or documents
5. **Escalate daily after Day 5**: If access is not provisioned within the target window, escalate to the client contact daily
6. **Document everything**: Record the date each access was requested, granted, and verified
