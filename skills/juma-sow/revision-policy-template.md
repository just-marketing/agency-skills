## Revision Definitions

- A "revision round" consists of consolidated feedback from a single point of contact. Multiple conflicting feedback sources submitted separately count as multiple rounds.
- Revisions address changes within the original scope and brief. Requests that alter the fundamental direction, strategy, or scope of a deliverable constitute a new deliverable and will be handled through the change order process.
- Feedback not received within [X] business days of deliverable submission constitutes approval.

## Key Principles

- **Always define exact numbers** of revision rounds per deliverable type
- **Require consolidated feedback** -- specify that feedback from a single point of contact counts as one round
- **Price additional rounds explicitly** -- this protects the agency and forces the client to provide organized, thoughtful feedback
- **Set a feedback deadline** -- auto-approval after a defined period prevents indefinite review cycles
- **Distinguish revisions from scope changes** -- changes in direction go through the change order process, not the revision process
