### Sustain Phase (Week 3-6)

## Budget Reallocation Rules

| Trigger | Condition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| **Underperformance** | [Channel] CPA exceeds $[threshold] for [X] consecutive days | Reduce budget by [X%], reallocate to top performer |
| **Overperformance** | [Channel] CPA is below $[target] with room to scale | Increase budget by [X%] from contingency or underperformer |
| **Budget exhaustion** | [Channel] consistently maxes daily budget before end of day | Increase daily cap by [X%] if performance supports it |
| **Diminishing returns** | [Channel] frequency exceeds [X] or CPM increases by [X%] | Reduce budget, refresh creative, or shift to new audience |
