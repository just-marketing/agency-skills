# Content Citability Scorecard

Use this scorecard to evaluate the client's website content for characteristics that make it likely to be cited by LLMs. Each factor is scored 1-10, and the average produces the overall Content Citability Score.

---

## Citability Factors

### 1. Clear, Structured Answers
**Question:** Does the content provide direct, concise answers to common questions?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | Content is vague, meandering, or requires significant reading to extract answers |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Answers are present but buried in long-form content without clear formatting |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Direct answers are prominently placed, well-structured with headers, and easy to extract |

### 2. Factual Density
**Question:** Does the content include specific data points, statistics, and verifiable claims?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | Content is opinion-based with few or no data points |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Some statistics and data points present but not consistently sourced |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Rich in specific data, statistics, and verifiable claims with sources cited |

### 3. Authority Signals (E-E-A-T)
**Question:** Does the content demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | No author attribution, no credentials, no expert citations |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Some author bios present, occasional expert references |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Clear author bios with credentials, expert quotes, original research, methodology transparency |

### 4. Structured Data Markup
**Question:** Is schema.org markup implemented to help machines parse the content?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | No structured data or only basic Organization schema |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Some schema types implemented but incomplete or with errors |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Comprehensive, error-free schema markup covering all relevant content types |

### 5. Content Freshness
**Question:** Is the content regularly updated with current information?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | Most content is 12+ months old with no visible update dates |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Mix of fresh and stale content; some pages updated regularly |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Content is consistently updated, dates are visible, and information reflects current state |

### 6. Unique Value
**Question:** Does the content offer original insights, data, or perspectives not found elsewhere?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | Content is generic and duplicates widely available information |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Some original perspective but largely similar to competitor content |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Original research, proprietary data, unique frameworks, or expert perspectives that are not available elsewhere |

### 7. Format Accessibility
**Question:** Is the content organized with clear headings, lists, tables, and summaries that LLMs can easily extract?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | Wall-of-text formatting, no clear hierarchy, poor use of HTML structure |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Some use of headings and lists but inconsistent across the site |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Consistent use of H2-H4 hierarchy, bulleted/numbered lists, tables, summary sections, and TL;DR blocks |

### 8. Topic Coverage Breadth
**Question:** Does the site comprehensively cover its category, or are there significant gaps?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|---------|
| 1-3 (Weak) | Covers only a narrow slice of the topic area; major gaps in category coverage |
| 4-6 (Adequate) | Covers core topics but lacks depth or misses adjacent/supporting topics |
| 7-10 (Strong) | Comprehensive topical authority with pillar content, supporting articles, and coverage of edge cases |

## Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Assessment | Implication |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **8-10** | Highly Citable | Content is well-positioned for AI citation. Focus on maintaining freshness and building authority. |
| **5-7** | Moderately Citable | Content has potential but needs targeted optimization to improve citation likelihood. |
| **1-4** | Low Citability | Significant content restructuring and creation needed before AI search visibility will improve. |
