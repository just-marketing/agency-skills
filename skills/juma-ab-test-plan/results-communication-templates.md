# Results Communication Templates

Use these templates to communicate A/B test results to clients in non-technical language. Choose the appropriate template based on the test outcome.

## Win Template

> "We tested [change] against the original [page/element]. After [N] visitors over [N] weeks, the new version increased [metric] by [X%], which translates to approximately [Y] additional [conversions/revenue] per month. We recommend implementing this change permanently."

### Win Template Example

> "We tested a shorter 4-field lead form against the original 8-field version on the demo request page. After 28,000 visitors over 3 weeks, the shorter form increased completion rate by 18%, which translates to approximately 45 additional demo requests per month. We recommend implementing the shorter form permanently."

---

## Loss Template

> "We tested [change] against the original [page/element]. After [N] visitors over [N] weeks, the original performed better. This is still a valuable result -- we now know [what we learned] and can apply this insight to [next test]. Our next test will focus on [next hypothesis]."

### Loss Template Example

> "We tested removing the pricing table from the landing page against the original version with pricing visible. After 15,000 visitors over 4 weeks, the original page with visible pricing performed better. This is still a valuable result -- we now know that price transparency builds trust with this audience rather than creating friction. Our next test will focus on reformatting the pricing table to highlight the most popular plan."

---

## Inconclusive Template

> "We tested [change] over [N] weeks but didn't see a statistically meaningful difference. This means [change] likely doesn't have a large impact on [metric]. We recommend [next action: extend test, try bigger change, move to next test]."

### Inconclusive Template Example

> "We tested a green CTA button against the original blue button over 4 weeks but didn't see a statistically meaningful difference. This means button color likely doesn't have a large impact on click-through rate for this page. We recommend moving on to test the CTA copy itself, which is more likely to influence visitor behavior."

---

## Communication Best Practices

1. **Lead with the outcome**: State the result in the first sentence
2. **Translate to business impact**: Convert percentage changes to real numbers (leads, revenue, customers)
3. **Frame losses and inconclusive results as learnings**: Every test generates knowledge
4. **Always include the next step**: What happens next based on this result?
5. **Avoid statistical jargon**: Say "meaningful difference" not "statistically significant at p < 0.05"
6. **Include the timeline and sample size**: Builds credibility that the test was properly run
7. **Never present inconclusive as a failure**: Knowing that something does not matter is valuable information that prevents wasted effort
