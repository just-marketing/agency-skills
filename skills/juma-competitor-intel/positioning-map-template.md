# Positioning Map Template

The positioning map plots the client and competitors on two strategic axes to visualize competitive whitespace and positioning opportunities.

## Map Structure

```
[Axis Label: e.g., Technical -------> Accessible]

                HIGH [Axis 2, e.g., Premium]
                    |
    [Comp A]        |          [Comp B]
                    |
                    |     [Client]
                    |
LOW [Axis 1] ------+------ HIGH [Axis 1]
                    |
         [Comp C]   |
                    |    [Comp D]
                    |
                LOW [Axis 2, e.g., Value]
```

**Positioning Interpretation:** [2-3 sentences explaining where the client sits relative to competitors and what whitespace exists on the map.]

## How to Choose Axes

Select two axes that are:
1. **Relevant to the industry** -- dimensions that matter to buyers in this market
2. **Differentiating** -- axes that spread competitors apart rather than clustering them
3. **Actionable** -- dimensions the client can deliberately move along

### Common Axis Pairs by Industry

| Industry | Axis 1 | Axis 2 |
|----------|--------|--------|
| B2B SaaS | Enterprise <-> SMB | Technical <-> User-Friendly |
| E-commerce | Premium <-> Value | Curated <-> Broad Selection |
| Professional Services | Specialized <-> Full-Service | Relationship-Driven <-> Technology-Driven |
| Healthcare | Clinical <-> Consumer-Friendly | Local/Regional <-> National |
| Financial Services | Traditional <-> Digital-First | Mass Market <-> High-Net-Worth |
| Education | Academic <-> Practical | Self-Paced <-> Instructor-Led |

### Custom Axis Ideas

- **Price/Value:** Budget <-> Premium
- **Brand Personality:** Corporate <-> Playful
- **Audience Sophistication:** Beginner <-> Expert
- **Service Model:** Self-Serve <-> High-Touch
- **Innovation:** Established/Traditional <-> Cutting-Edge
- **Scope:** Niche/Specialized <-> Broad/Full-Suite

## Interpreting the Map

When writing the positioning interpretation, address:

1. **Where the client sits** -- describe their current position clearly
2. **Cluster analysis** -- are competitors clustered together, leaving whitespace?
3. **Whitespace opportunities** -- are there underserved quadrants the client could own?
4. **Competitive threats** -- which competitors are closest to the client's position?
5. **Strategic direction** -- should the client reinforce their current position or shift?

## Connecting to Recommendations

The positioning map should directly inform:
- **Messaging strategy** -- how to emphasize the client's unique position
- **Content strategy** -- topics and angles that reinforce the desired position
- **Channel strategy** -- channels that align with the client's positioning quadrant
- **Competitive counter-positioning** -- how to differentiate from nearby competitors
