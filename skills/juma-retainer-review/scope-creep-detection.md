# Scope Creep Detection

Use this framework to identify, categorize, and quantify out-of-scope work on retainer accounts.

## Scope Creep Categories

### 1. Recurring Scope Creep
Work done regularly that is not in the SOW.

**Examples:**
- Weekly report that was supposed to be monthly
- Managing a social channel that is not in scope
- Attending meetings not included in the retainer
- Providing creative services when only strategy is contracted

**Tracking Template:**

| Activity | Not in SOW | Est. Hours/Month | Duration | Total Uncompensated Hours |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|----------|--------------------------|
| [Activity] | [Correct] | [X] | [X months] | [X] |
| [Activity] | [Correct] | [X] | [X months] | [X] |

---

### 2. Ad Hoc Scope Creep
One-off requests that accumulate over time.

**Examples:**
- "Quick favor" design requests
- Unplanned strategy calls
- Additional platform management not in scope
- One-off reports or analyses for client's internal meetings
- Handling vendor relationships on the client's behalf

**Tracking Template:**

- [Date]: [Description of request, estimated hours spent]
- [Date]: [Description of request, estimated hours spent]
- [Date]: [Description of request, estimated hours spent]

---

### 3. Complexity Creep
In-scope deliverables that have grown more complex than originally scoped.

**Examples:**
- Monthly report that now takes 6 hours instead of the scoped 2 hours due to added sections
- Blog posts that require extensive research and SME interviews when scoped as standard articles
- Campaign management that now covers 3x the number of ad groups originally planned
- Social media management where the client has added 2 new platforms since the SOW was signed

**Tracking Template:**

| Deliverable | Originally Scoped Hours | Current Avg Hours | Reason for Increase |
|-------------|------------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| [Deliverable] | [X] | [X] | [Explanation] |
| [Deliverable] | [X] | [X] | [Explanation] |

## Documentation for Renegotiation

When preparing for a scope renegotiation, compile:

1. **Specific examples with dates**: "On [date], [specific request] took [X hours] and is not covered by the current SOW"
2. **Recurring patterns**: "For the last [X months], we have been delivering [activity] weekly, which is not in the SOW and represents [X hours/month]"
3. **Complexity growth**: "[Deliverable] was originally scoped at [X hours] but now consistently takes [Y hours] due to [reason]"
4. **Total financial impact**: "Over the last [X months], uncompensated out-of-scope work totals [X hours] valued at $[amount]"

## Prevention Checklist

- [ ] SOW clearly defines all deliverables with frequency and complexity expectations
- [ ] Hours are tracked per deliverable (not just per client) to catch overruns early
- [ ] Account managers flag out-of-scope requests when they happen, not months later
- [ ] A change order process exists for significant scope additions
- [ ] Monthly internal review compares actual work performed to SOW deliverables
