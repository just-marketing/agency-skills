# Platform Selection Guide

Use this matrix to evaluate and select paid media platforms based on client objectives, audience, and budget.

## Platform Comparison Matrix

| Platform | Best For | Consider When |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Google Search** | High-intent capture | Client's category has search volume |
| **Google Display/YouTube** | Awareness, retargeting | Need broad reach or video storytelling |
| **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** | B2C, ecommerce, lead gen | Visual products, broad targeting |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, professional services | Targeting by job title, company, industry |
| **TikTok** | Gen Z/Millennial, awareness | Brand has or can create short-form video |
| **Programmatic** | Scale, retargeting | Need reach beyond walled gardens |
| **Microsoft Ads** | B2B, desktop-heavy audiences | Lower CPCs, older demographic |

## Platform Selection Process

For each selected platform, document:
1. **Why it was chosen**: Which client objective does it serve?
2. **Role in the media mix**: Awareness, consideration, conversion, or retargeting?
3. **Budget allocation rationale**: Why this percentage of total spend?
4. **Expected performance**: Target CPA/ROAS/CPM based on benchmarks
5. **Minimum viable budget**: The floor below which the platform cannot generate reliable data

## Platform-Objective Alignment

| Objective | Primary Platforms | Secondary Platforms |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Brand Awareness** | Google Display/YouTube, TikTok, Programmatic | Meta, LinkedIn |
| **Lead Generation** | Google Search, Meta, LinkedIn | Microsoft Ads |
| **Ecommerce Sales** | Google Search, Meta, Google Shopping | TikTok, Programmatic |
| **B2B Demand Gen** | LinkedIn, Google Search | Microsoft Ads, Programmatic |
| **App Installs** | Meta, Google UAC, TikTok | Programmatic |
| **Local Business** | Google Search, Meta | Microsoft Ads |
| **Retargeting** | Meta, Google Display, Programmatic | LinkedIn, TikTok |

## Minimum Budget Guidance

| Platform | Minimum Monthly Spend | Rationale |
|----------|----------------------|-----------|
| Google Search | $1,500-3,000 | Need enough clicks to optimize toward conversions |
| Google Display/YouTube | $2,000-5,000 | Broad reach requires minimum impression volume |
| Meta | $1,500-3,000 | Algorithm needs ~50 conversions/week for optimization |
| LinkedIn | $3,000-5,000 | Higher CPCs/CPMs require larger budget for meaningful data |
| TikTok | $1,500-3,000 | Similar to Meta in optimization requirements |
| Programmatic | $5,000+ | Broad reach platform needs scale to perform |
| Microsoft Ads | $1,000-2,000 | Lower CPCs mean less budget needed for data |

These are general guidelines. Actual minimums depend on industry, geography, and competition.
