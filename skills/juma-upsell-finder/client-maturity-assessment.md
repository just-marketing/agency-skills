# Client Maturity Assessment

Use this framework to evaluate a client's marketing maturity before recommending new services. Pitching services beyond a client's maturity level wastes credibility.

## Maturity Levels

### Foundational
**Characteristics:**
- Basic analytics in place but not fully configured
- Running 1-2 marketing channels
- Limited or no conversion tracking
- No formal testing or optimization program
- Small marketing team or no dedicated marketing staff

**Ready for:**
- Analytics setup and configuration
- Basic SEO (technical fixes, on-page optimization)
- Single-channel paid media (Google Search or Meta)
- Content marketing fundamentals (blog, basic social)
- Email marketing setup

**Not yet ready for:**
- Multi-touch attribution modeling
- Programmatic advertising
- Advanced A/B testing programs
- Marketing automation
- Account-based marketing (ABM)

---

### Intermediate
**Characteristics:**
- Clean analytics with conversion tracking
- Active on 3-4 marketing channels
- Some testing history (even informal)
- Dedicated marketing contact or small team
- Consistent content production
- Basic reporting cadence established

**Ready for:**
- Multi-channel paid media strategy
- CRO and structured A/B testing
- Content strategy with pillar planning
- SEO strategy (beyond technical fixes)
- Social media strategy (beyond posting)
- Email automation and segmentation
- Competitive intelligence

**Not yet ready for:**
- Complex attribution modeling
- Large-scale programmatic
- AI/ML-driven personalization
- Enterprise ABM platforms

---

### Advanced
**Characteristics:**
- Sophisticated analytics with custom tracking
- Active on 5+ channels with cross-channel coordination
- Established testing culture and optimization cadence
- Larger marketing team with specialized roles
- Data-driven decision-making is the norm
- Strong content engine producing regularly

**Ready for:**
- Advanced attribution and marketing mix modeling
- Programmatic and connected TV
- AI-powered personalization
- Account-based marketing
- Full-funnel reporting and revenue attribution
- Influencer and partnership programs
- International/multi-market expansion

---

## Assessment Checklist

| Dimension | Foundational | Intermediate | Advanced |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Analytics | Basic GA installed | GA4 + GTM + conversion tracking | Custom events + attribution + data warehouse |
| Channels | 1-2 active | 3-4 active | 5+ coordinated |
| Testing | None | Some A/B tests run | Structured testing roadmap |
| Content | Sporadic | Consistent cadence | Pillar strategy + repurposing |
| Team | No dedicated marketing | Small team or dedicated contact | Specialized roles |
| Data usage | Gut feel | Reviews reports monthly | Data-driven decisions weekly |

**Overall maturity level:** [Foundational / Intermediate / Advanced]
